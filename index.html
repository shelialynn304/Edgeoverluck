Tote Board Scanner FAQ

Does the Tote Board Scanner predict winners?

No. It provides math analysis of displayed odds — implied probability, effective takeout, and fair odds — not picks or guarantees.

Why is implied probability shown as a range on tote boards?

Tote board odds are displayed rounded down. A board showing 5/2 could reflect true odds anywhere from 2.5 to just under 3, so the tool shows the honest range instead of a false-precision single number.

Are exotic ticket costs exact?

Yes. Exacta, trifecta, and superfecta box, key, and wheel costs are computed by enumerating every valid combination, not shortcut formulas, so overlapping position groups are always counted correctly.

Is a scan a live price?

No. A scan is a timestamped snapshot. Tote board odds move until post, so treat every result as a moment in time, not a locked-in price.
